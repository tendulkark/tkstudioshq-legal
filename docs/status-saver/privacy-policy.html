Privacy Policy — Status Saver for WhatsApp

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Status Saver for WhatsApp ("the app") is developed and published independently. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to WhatsApp Inc. or Meta Platforms, Inc. This policy explains what data the app accesses and why.

1. Folder access

The app asks you to pick a folder using Android's built-in folder picker (Storage Access Framework). It only reads image and video files inside the single folder you choose — typically WhatsApp's or WhatsApp Business's status media folder. The app:

  • never requests write access to that folder — it can only read what's already there;
  • never reads your chats, messages, contacts, or any other app's data;
  • never uploads the media files you view, save, or share to any server the developer controls.

When you save or share a status, the file is copied using Android's own MediaStore/share APIs directly to your device's gallery or to the app you choose to share with — it never passes through, or is stored on, any server operated by this app.

2. Analytics and crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (both Google services) to understand feature usage (e.g. which screens are opened, whether a download succeeded) and to capture crash/error diagnostics so bugs can be fixed. This data is associated with an anonymous, app-level installation identifier — not with your name, phone number, or WhatsApp account, none of which the app ever has access to.

3. Advertising

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID and other device signals to serve and measure ads, and — where you have given consent — to personalize them.

Where required by law (including the EU/EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before any personalized ad is requested. You can review or change that choice at any time from the app's overflow menu (⋮ → Privacy Options) when that option is shown.

See Google's own privacy policy for how it handles this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data this app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No contacts, phone number, or precise location.
  • No WhatsApp chat content, message history, or metadata.
  • No data is sold to third parties.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Material changes will also ship with an updated app version on the Play Store.

7. Contact
